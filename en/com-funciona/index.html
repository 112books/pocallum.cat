How it works

We like to document events by being part of them. We don’t turn up at the last minute asking where the pit is: we are there, and you can see it in the photographs.

This is what happens from the moment you write to us until you receive the archive.

1. You tell us about the project. Through the form or by email. The more context the better: what it is, when, where, and what you need the images for.

2. We reply within 48 hours. With a tailored quote: what it includes, what it doesn’t, and the price. If we’re not a good fit, we tell you anyway.

3. We hold the date. With a 30% deposit. Always invoiced.

4. We document. No flash where possible, without interrupting, with available light. For a festival we talk to production beforehand about where we can stand.

5. We deliver. An edited selection of 30 to 50 images per coverage, in 72 hours. A portfolio shoot, in one week. A festival, everything at the end (or daily, if you want it earlier).

6. Clear rights. Use for communication, press, website, social media and the client’s archive, written into the quote. No surprises, no small print.
