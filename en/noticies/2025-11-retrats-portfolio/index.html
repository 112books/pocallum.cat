Portraits to start the year looking sharp (and with a better portfolio)

Two portrait session options for artists who need to refresh their professional images.

If you’re an artist — musician, actor, writer, poet, visual creator, performer, or any other stage creature — you know that a strong image is half the battle. Refreshing it every now and then won’t hurt; it might even give you a boost.

That’s why I’ve prepared two options to help you start the year with photographs that truly speak for you.

Option 1 — Analogue Black & White Portrait (6×6 format)

From 150 €

Up to 10 high-resolution photographs, with all the texture and charm of classic film photography.

Option 2 — Digital Portrait

From 200 €

Up to 25 final images: sharp, versatile, and ready for any professional use.

All sessions include

  • Up to 1 hour of shooting
  • Up to 2 wardrobe changes
  • Flexible scheduling
  • Delivery of all images in high resolution

Optional services

  • Prints on paper (pricing depends on size and paper type)
  • Hair, makeup or styling services available; contacts can be provided if needed

Why now?

Because a new year deserves a new image. Because gigs, castings and presentations don’t wait. And because — let’s be honest — updating that 2018 portrait won’t hurt anyone.

I’ll bring the camera, you bring the attitude.
