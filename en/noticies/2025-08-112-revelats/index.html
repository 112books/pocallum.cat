The first challenge of 112 Revelats gets underway: 'Arrencant el dia'

112 Revelats is an initiative by 112Books to give visibility to emerging photographers through collective work.

We’re launching the first challenge of 112 Revelats, an initiative by 112Books to give visibility to emerging photographers through collective work.

The goal is to grow a creative community that transforms photographs into shared narratives. The work produced will eventually be compiled into a printed book.

The first challenge: Arrencant el dia

The first challenge is titled Arrencant el dia (Starting the Day). The call is open to anyone who wants to take part in building a shared photographic narrative.

Full conditions and the participation form are available at 112revelats.112books.eu.

How to take part

  • Review the challenge guidelines on the website
  • Submit your proposal through the participation form
  • Contribute photographs aligned with the challenge theme

Sponsorships

The project accepts sponsors. If you want these photographic stories to reach printed form, you can help make it possible through the project website.

See you in the darkroom.
