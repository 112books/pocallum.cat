Guide to Photography Exhibitions and Spaces in Barcelona

A curated list of the best venues in Barcelona and the metropolitan area where you can regularly see photography exhibitions, with direct links to their current programming.

Barcelona, a city of contrasts, offers a rich and diverse cultural panorama, especially in the field of photography. This guide presents the best venues to enjoy photography in Barcelona, based on years of personal experience visiting galleries, art centres and civic spaces.

Featured exhibition spaces

  • Centre d’Art Santa Mònica
    Key venue for contemporary and experimental practices in image and photography.

  • Foto Colectania
    Private foundation focused on contemporary Iberian photography and collecting.

  • KBr Fundación MAPFRE – Photography Centre
    International reference centre dedicated exclusively to photography.

  • CCCB – Centre for Contemporary Culture of Barcelona
    Documentary photography, visual essay and image culture.

  • MACBA – Barcelona Museum of Contemporary Art
    Photography within contemporary art and conceptual practices.

  • CaixaForum Barcelona
    Major photography exhibitions with an educational approach.

  • Fabra i Coats – Contemporary Art Centre
    Large-format photography within hybrid contemporary practices.

  • Museu Nacional d’Art de Catalunya (MNAC)
    Historical and heritage photography.

  • Museum of the History of Catalonia
    Documentary photography and historical memory.

  • MUHBA – Barcelona History Museum
    Photography as a document of urban memory.

  • Photographic Social Vision
    Non-profit organisation committed to the social value of documentary photography and photojournalism.

Galleries and civic centres

  • Pati Llimona Civic Centre
    Steady programme of documentary and emerging photography.

  • Nau Bostik
    Industrial space with urban and experimental photography.

  • Can Basté Civic Centre
    Photography hub in Nou Barris with exhibitions, workshops and residencies.

  • Casa Elizalde Civic Centre
    Regular contemporary photography exhibitions.

  • Casa Golferichs Civic Centre – Francesc Català-Roca
    Photography and visual culture with a steady programme.

  • Hangar
    Art production centre with experimental photography.

  • Barcelona Photographic Archive
    Preservation and exhibitions of historical photography.

  • Barcelona Historical Archive
    Documentary photography and historical research.

Photography schools with exhibitions

  • IEFC – Institut d’Estudis Fotogràfics de Catalunya
    End-of-year exhibitions and author projects.

  • IDEP Barcelona
    Student project exhibitions and emerging professionals.

  • Elisava
    Contemporary photography linked to design and new visual languages.

  • Serra i Abella School
    Public centre with a strong photography tradition.

  • Llumàtics
    Chemical photography training centre.

Photography festivals

  • La Nuu (Rubí)
    International photography festival turning the city into an open exhibition circuit.

  • Revela’t (Vilassar de Dalt)
    International festival of analogue photography and alternative processes.

  • DOCfield Barcelona
    Festival of documentary photography and contemporary social narrative.

  • KBr FLAMA
    Emerging talent programme linked to KBr Fundación MAPFRE.

  • SCAN Tarragona
    Contemporary photography festival with an institutional curatorial focus.

  • Panoràmic (Granollers)
    Photography, film and new media in a hybrid format.

  • Visa pour l’Image (Perpignan)
    International photojournalism festival, Europe’s benchmark.

This guide is meant to be a dynamic resource. If you think a space is missing, write to us and we will add it.

About the author

Joan Linux Martínez — Photographer

Live music and performing arts photographer based in Barcelona since 2002. He has documented hundreds of concerts, theatre premieres and cultural festivals across Catalonia. He publishes photobooks and works regularly with the Festival de Blues de Barcelona, Jazz I Am, Nou Barris Meets New Orleans and many others.
