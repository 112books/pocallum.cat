What we do

Photography services

We don't work with fixed rates. Each project is different and deserves a tailored quote.

Culture

The atmosphere of a jazz concert at 2am. The tension before going on stage. Dance in motion. We photograph what happens when culture breathes.

Concerts and music events

Documentary photography of music events: jazz, blues, classical, rock, folk and anything that sounds good. We capture the energy on stage and the atmosphere of the venue.

Bands and musicians

Photo sessions for bands and musical artists: press photos, social media, album covers and promo material. At your space or ours.

Performing arts

Theatre, dance, circus, performance. We photograph rehearsals and shows. We understand stage dynamics and know when to shoot without disturbing the artist.

Artists

Photos that show who you really are. For your professional career, your dossier, your digital presence.

Actor portfolio

Professional photo book for actors and actresses. Studio and location series. We cater to the specific needs of the theatrical and audiovisual world.

Artist portfolio

Photo session for artists of any discipline: musicians, dancers, illustrators, performers. A book that speaks of your work and your identity.

Professional headshot

A single photograph that represents who you are and what you do. For your website, LinkedIn, press or any professional use. Natural, direct, no effects.

Companies

Photography as a communication tool for companies, organisations and cultural entities. Images that explain what you do and how you do it.

Team and premises

Corporate photography of your team and work spaces. For your website, annual report or presentation materials.

Social media photography

Periodic photo content sessions for Instagram, LinkedIn and other platforms. Images consistent with your visual identity.
