What we do

Photography services

We don't work with fixed rates. Each project is different and deserves a tailored quote.

01

The Moment

Live photojournalism. Reactivity, adrenaline, the moment that won't come back. Analogue preferred, digital when needed. Always with the same eye.

  • Concerts and live performances Jazz, blues, flamenco, punk, folk, classical — anything that sounds real and live. We capture the energy on stage and the atmosphere of the venue: what happens on stage and what happens in the audience.
  • Festivals We've worked at intimate festivals of twenty people and events of thousands. We understand the dynamics of a festival: the structure, the surprises, the moments that don't appear in the programme.
  • Cultural events Vernissages, presentations, poetry recitals, readings, one-off performances. Culture happens constantly and often nobody documents it. We do.
Let's talk →
02

The Production

Here the moment stops. We study the light, build the frame, think before shooting. For musicians, companies and creators who need images that last.

  • Band and artist photography Album covers, press material, social media content, album recording behind-the-scenes. Sessions designed for the specific use you need, not for stock.
  • Performing arts Dance, theatre, circus, performance. Proper press kits, company books, images for programmers, agents and festivals. We photograph rehearsals and shows. We know when to shoot without disturbing.
  • Visual arts and editorial commissions Documentation of work for painters, sculptors and video artists. Book covers. Specific editorial commissions. Anything that requires stopping time and thinking.
Let's talk →
03

The Paper

In synergy with 112books.eu, an independent photobook publisher. From concert to book. From record to object. From tour to tangible memory.

  • Individual and project photobooks From idea to final product. Support at every stage: concept, image editing, design, printing, distribution.
  • Limited collector's editions Short runs, selected materials, objects worth owning. The vinyl collector and the analogue photography collector are the same person.
Let's talk →
04

The Person

The image that lets you be who you are. Not the one you're supposed to look like.

  • Actor portfolio For actors and actresses who need material for casting directors, agents and programmers. Studio and location series. We understand the specific needs of the theatrical and audiovisual circuit.
  • Artist portfolio For musicians, dancers, performers, illustrators and creators of any discipline. A book that speaks of your work and your identity, not an archetype of how an artist should look.
  • Professional headshot A photograph that represents who you are and what you do. For your website, LinkedIn, press or any professional use. Natural, direct. No effects, no curtain backgrounds.
Let's talk →
05

The Cultural Organisation

For companies and organisations that understand culture. Images that explain what you do and how you do it, without forced smiles or corporate rhetoric.

  • Team and premises Photography of your team and workspaces. For your website, annual report or presentation materials. No neutral studio backgrounds if your workplace is interesting.
  • Social media content Periodic photography sessions for Instagram, LinkedIn and other platforms. Images consistent with your visual identity. A digital presence that breathes.
