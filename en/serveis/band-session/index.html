What we do

Band and artist photography session in Barcelona

Band or solo artist photography session in Barcelona. Images for press, social media, album covers and promoter presentations. No generic aesthetics.

There are sessions that look like clothing catalogues with musicians as models. That’s not what we do. The image should sound like the music: if the band plays Delta blues and the photos look like a cologne ad, something has gone wrong. We know music from the inside — and it shows in the pictures.

Solo artist or band?

We work with individual artists and groups of any size. For bands, we don’t line people up looking at the camera: we document the real dynamic, the energy, the relationship between members.

What’s included?

  • 2 to 3-hour session in Barcelona (concert venue, street, studio or agreed location)
  • Selection of 20 edited high-resolution photographs
  • Unrestricted licence for all professional uses
  • Delivery within 5 working days
  • Optional: analogue version on 35mm film or medium format

How does the process work?

We talk first. You tell us about the project, where you are in your career, whether there’s an album or tour behind it. We choose aesthetics, lighting and locations based on the sound. There’s no standard format.

What does it cost?

Depends on duration, number of people and whether you want an analogue version. Write to us and we’ll send a proposal within 48 hours.
