What we do

Limited collector's editions

We turn material from a festival, event or working sessions into a tangible photobook. An object you can touch, gift or sell. In collaboration with 112books.eu.

We cover the event —or work from existing material— and the final result is a photobook: a physical editorial object with weight and intent.

In a fully digital age, where we don’t know for certain which images will last, a memory turned into a tangible photographic product —something you can actually touch— adds real value to the whole production. It becomes a remarkable keepsake, ready to give as a gift or to sell.

How does it work?

We can start from two situations:

  • Event coverage — we attend the festival, residency, premiere or sessions and document it. The resulting material is the basis for the photobook.
  • Existing material — you already have the photographs and want to turn them into an editorial object. We edit, sequence and produce.

Either way the result is the same: a custom photobook, printed on paper, in a limited edition.

An example: the Antropoformologies collection

The Antropoformologies trilogy by 112books shows what’s possible: three volumes documenting a long-running photographic project, edited and printed as collector’s objects. A collection that is just beginning.

Who is this for?

Festivals, cultural spaces, theatre or dance companies, musicians with an album or tour, visual artists, institutions. Any project that deserves to exist on paper.

Who do we work with?

In collaboration with 112books.eu, a publishing house specialising in art printing. Formats, papers, binding: from pocket size to 30×30 cm. Single-copy editions or limited collector’s runs.

What does it cost?

Depends on format, number of images and print run. Write to us with your project and we’ll send a proposal within 48 hours.
