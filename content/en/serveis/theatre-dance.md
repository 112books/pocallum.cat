## Working conditions

Dark auditorium, aggressive backlighting, constant movement and one take with no chance to repeat. What would be a problem for others is our normal working environment. We've been doing this for years.

## Who do we work with?

We've documented theatre premieres, contemporary dance shows, new circus productions and visual arts performance. We work as comfortably on a large-format stage as in a forty-seat alternative space.

## Rehearsals, premieres and tours

We can document full dress rehearsals (fewer restrictions, better access), premieres (maximum emotional charge) or full tours. Each mode has its own logic and its own price.

## What does it cost?

Depends on format, access and number of sessions. Write to us and we'll send a proposal within 48 hours.
