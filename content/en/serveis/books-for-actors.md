Your portfolio is your first impression. Casting directors, agencies and producers look at it for a few seconds: either it hooks them or it doesn't. A white backdrop and studio lighting won't tell your story. We will.

## What does an actor portfolio with Pocallum include?

We work with natural or reportage light — never generic studio setups — to capture your real range. The result is a set of photographs that show character, expressive depth and presence, not a series of poses.

- 2-hour session at Barcelona locations
- Selection of 20 edited photographs in high resolution
- Unrestricted licence (castings, websites, social media, agencies)
- Delivery within 5 working days
- Optional: analogue version on 35mm film or medium format

## Who is it for?

For actors and actresses at any stage of their career — whether you need your first professional portfolio or want to refresh your image for a new agency or new chapter.

## How does the session work?

We talk first: you tell us where you are in your career, which registers you want to highlight, and whether there's a specific casting or project involved. From there we decide locations, lighting and style. There's no fixed format — each portfolio is built around the person in front of the camera.

## What does it cost?

We don't work with fixed rates. The quote depends on the duration, number of locations and whether you want advanced editing or an analogue version. Write to us and we'll send a proposal within 48 hours.
