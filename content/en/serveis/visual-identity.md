The visual identity of an artistic project isn't a logo and a colour palette. It's the way the project presents itself to the world: the album cover, the concert poster, the social media images, the press kit. Everything has to speak the same language.

## A single piece or a full identity?

We can create one specific piece — a poster, an album cover, a visual for the tour — or build a coherent visual identity for a long-running project. We work according to what you need, not a fixed package.

## What kind of projects?

Albums, tours, theatre and dance shows, festivals, concert cycles, publications, cultural institutions. Any scale.

## How does the process work?

We start by understanding the project: the sound, the show, the audience, the references. We propose creative directions and work until the result is coherent with what you want to communicate. There's no fixed number of revisions.

## What does it cost?

Depends on scope: one piece or a full visual system. Write to us with project details and we'll send a proposal within 48 hours.
