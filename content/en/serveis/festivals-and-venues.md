## What does coverage include?

- Performances on stage and off-stage moments
- The audience, the atmosphere, the setting
- The people who make the event happen: crew, artists, organisers
- Night-time moments, difficult light, transitions between spaces

## An archive that lasts

The images we deliver are organised by day and edition, with an unrestricted professional licence. Built to be reused: website, press, social media, grant applications, annual reports. A good photographic archive is a long-term investment.

## Experience across years and disciplines

We've worked with long-running jazz and blues festivals, theatre fairs, dance cycles and cultural spaces in Barcelona and across the region. We know the rhythms of a festival because we've lived them from the inside, year after year.

## What does it cost?

Depends on the number of days, access and volume of edited images. Write to us and we'll send a proposal within 48 hours.
