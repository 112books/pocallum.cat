We have been documenting live culture in Barcelona since 2002. Jazz at the Jamboree, blues in Nou Barris, flamenco on the street, theatre in the neighbourhood hall next door. Performing arts of every kind, in every format, with whatever light — or darkness — the situation demands.

## Cultural photography in Barcelona

Work with us if you need images that tell the truth about what you do. [Concert photography](/en/serveis/live-music/) for press and social media. [Actor portfolios](/en/serveis/books-for-actors/) and [musician portfolios](/en/serveis/artistic-portfolios/). [Full festival and event coverage](/en/serveis/festivals-and-venues/) across Barcelona and Catalonia. [Editorial design](/en/serveis/album-cover/) for albums, posters and publications.

We are specialists in jazz photography in Barcelona, in blues, flamenco, theatre and dance photography. Years in the pit and the stalls — and it shows in the photographs.

Our base is at Nau Bostik, in the Segrera neighbourhood. The analogue darkroom is here. Most projects begin with a conversation.
