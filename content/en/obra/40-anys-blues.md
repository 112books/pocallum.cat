Four decades of blues in Barcelona, in portraits. The project started in 2020, photographing every protagonist of the scene — musicians, venues and the temples of local blues — and this book is the result: the definitive edition. In 2022 we had published an advance, «Preses Falses».

We presented it on **6 November 2026**. If you missed it, here is the [video summary](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_6aDeOZyhQ).

The book can be bought, and the whole project — musicians, history and venues of Barcelona blues — is at [40anysblues.112books.eu](https://40anysblues.112books.eu).
