Barcelona blues has forty years of history behind it and, since 6 November, it also has the book it deserves. «40 anys de blues a Barcelona» gathers portraits of around 150 musicians from the scene, shot on film —large format included— over more than five years of work. The texts are by Manolo Poy and the prologue by David Moreu.

We presented it on **6 November 2026** at the Centre Cultural Collblanc – La Torrassa (L'Hospitalet de Llobregat), at an event that also opened the project's photography exhibition. If you could not make it, our friends at Bad Music Blues recorded it: [video of the presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7_6aDeOZyhQ).

## How to get it

The book can be bought, and the whole project —each musician's page, the history of the scene and the venues of blues in the city— can be explored at **[40anysblues.112books.eu](https://40anysblues.112books.eu/)**. The site stays live.

- **Price:** €38. Members of the Societat de Blues de Barcelona: €33.
- **Edition:** a short first print run.

There is also a page for the book on this site: [40 Anys de Blues a Barcelona](/en/obra/40-anys-blues/).
