Following a band around for a whole day is a privilege. On July 25, thanks to Ivan Kovačević's trust, I got to do it with the [Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra](https://bgko.org) (BGKO), as part of the [Mas i Mas Festival](https://masimasfestival.com). A band that brings the folk music of the Balkans and Eastern Europe to the stage: festive, intense, full of energy.

I started the day with them at the [Here Comes The Sound](https://hctsound.com) studio, where they ran through the repertoire before the show. There I met some of the special guests of the evening: [Ľubomír Gašpar](https://www.instagram.com/gaspar_music_/) on cimbalom, [Sasha Agranov](https://www.instagram.com/sasha_agranov/) on cello and legendary Hungarian violinist [Roby Lakatos](https://robylakatosviolin.com/). Hearing them from just a few metres away is a privilege hard to put into words.

But the most important part, for me, is the "meanwhile": everything that happens before the curtain goes up. The rehearsals, the conversations, the complicity, the setup, the soundcheck... Those hours make you understand that behind a two-hour concert there is a lot of invisible work.

{{< vimeo-embed "1213021190" "Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra — rehearsals" >}}

In the afternoon, we moved to the [Palau de la Música Catalana](https://www.palaumusica.cat). Setup, microphones, lighting, soundcheck. Just before seven, the audience began to fill their seats and the musicians retired to the dressing rooms to get ready for the stage.

{{< vimeo-embed "1213073113" "Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra — setup and soundcheck" >}}

The concert was memorable. It lasted nearly two hours, yet the repertoire went by surprisingly fast. The band started with a smaller lineup and, piece by piece, the rest of the musicians and the special guests joined in, turning the stage into a true celebration. Magali Sare and the magnificent Margherita Abita were the great vocal protagonists. When the encores came, the audience stood up: a packed Palau burst into a long ovation.

{{< vimeo-embed "1213075516" "Barcelona Gipsy balKan Orchestra — The concert" >}}

Afterwards, in the lobby, the musicians greeted the audience, signed records and thanked everyone for the warmth of the night. And as if Barcelona itself wanted to join the party, a heavy storm forced us to wait a while before leaving. A perfect ending to an intense day full of music and photographs.
