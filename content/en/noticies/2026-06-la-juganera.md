Some festivals exist because there is a budget. Others exist because there is a will. La Juganera is the second kind.

On Friday 29 May we were at Nau Bostik to cover the sixth edition. A courtyard, a lot of people, music that doesn't fit a single category, and the atmosphere a festival has when the same neighbourhood that organises it is the one that fills it.

We have added [La Juganera to our festivals section](/en/festivals/la-juganera/), with the editions we have documented since 2023 and the photo reports published on the blog.
