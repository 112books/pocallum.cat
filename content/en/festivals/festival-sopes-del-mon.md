Every autumn, Nou Barris fills with pots. The Festival de Sopes del Món — Festival of World Soups — proves that a simple bowl of soup can bring people together in ways no politics can manage. Organised by [9 Barris Acull](https://www.9barrisacull.org) and the Xarxa d'Intercanvi de Coneixements, it has now reached its nineteenth edition.

---

The festival brings together communities from across the neighbourhood — and the world — around a bowl of soup. Each participant brings their recipe, their story and their table. The result is a gathering that is as much political as gastronomic, in the best sense of both words.

We've been documenting it since 2013, covering editions from the 11th to the 19th, with the usual pilgrimage of camera, tripod and appetite.

## European artistic exchange

Our relationship with the project goes beyond festival photography. We have travelled to **Bologna** (Italy) and **Lille** (France) on several occasions as part of the artistic exchanges the project weaves with European organisations working on community culture and social inclusion.

Taking the camera to the international editions is another way of understanding what happens back here in Nou Barris.
