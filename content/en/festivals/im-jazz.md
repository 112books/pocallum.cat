I'm Jazz is the festival that changes format every year. New venues, new proposals, emerging names alongside established references. Barcelona jazz in a moment of permanent redefinition.

---

We've followed I'm Jazz since the early editions, and every year is different from the last. That's one of the things we love most: you never quite know where you're headed or what you'll find.

Unexpected venues. Groups you'd never heard of. A set that runs long because nobody wants it to end.

## Diversity as method

I'm Jazz doesn't try to be the best festival in the world. It tries to be the festival Barcelona needs: plural, generous, without hierarchies between the big names and the small ones.

The breadth of the scene gets concentrated into a few days that always end with the feeling they passed too fast. Some I'm Jazz sessions have turned out to be the images we most value from the entire year — and not always the ones with the headline guests.
