FITI is the festival of Alquimistes Teatre, one of the most established inclusive theatre companies in Spain. More than twenty-five years bringing mixed casts — actors with and without intellectual diversity — to the stage, from Santa Coloma de Gramenet to the world.

---

Alquimistes Teatre was founded in 1998 with a clear idea: inclusive theatre is not second-tier theatre or therapeutic theatre. It is theatre. And as such, it deserves proper stages, international festivals and photography that shows it as such.

FITI — Festival Internacional de Teatre Integratiu — is their biennial festival: a meeting point for companies from across the world working with mixed casts that demonstrate, performance after performance, that diversity on stage is not a handicap but an expressive strength.

## What we photograph there

Inclusive theatre is photographically unlike anything else: nothing can be predicted, bodies move differently, emotions surface unfiltered. It is the kind of performance that demands total presence.

Five consecutive editions — from the V to the IX — and each time we understand a little better that documenting FITI means documenting something that matters.
