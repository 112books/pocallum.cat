Every year, when the month of fasting ends, the Islamic community of Nou Barris takes to the streets. The Eid al-Fitr celebration is not an event for tourists or the press — it's a neighbourhood party, intimate and massive at once, filling squares and streets with families, food, music and prayer.

---

In 2023 we followed the customs of Nou Barris' Islamic community throughout the Eid al-Fitr festivities. A celebration the district's residents organise year after year, without posters or sponsors, driven purely by those who know exactly what they are celebrating.

Afternoon light, public spaces transformed, people who don't pose for the camera because they have better things to do. Exactly the kind of event we want to document.
