Among the finest festivals there are. All soul and small format. Proximity with the performers, with the organisation, and everything made with great care and love.

---

El Clot is a neighbourhood that has made blues its own. Clot amb Blues brings the music to the neighbourhood's own spaces — squares, venues, local facilities — with the intention of making music something that belongs to residents, not just to audiences.

Small in scale, large in content. Live concerts, street processions, proposals that go beyond the standard concert. The festival understands that neighbourhood music is, by definition, the kind that takes root deepest.

A joy to be part of. And a particular honour to have exhibited **"Preses Falses. 40 anys de Blues a Barcelona"** there in 2023, when the festival hosted the show at the Ateneu del Clot. Blues photography returning to blues concerts — in a neighbourhood venue, in front of an audience that truly loves the music — is exactly where it belonged.
