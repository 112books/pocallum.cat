El Clot is a neighbourhood that has made blues its own. Clot en Blues brings the music to the neighbourhood's own spaces — squares, venues, local facilities — with the intention of making music something that belongs to residents, not just to audiences.

---

The festival combines live concerts with proposals that go further: the photography exhibition **"Instants de Blues"** has accompanied some editions as a visual record of what music generates when it is photographed with the same attention given to listening.

In November 2024, Clot en Blues hosted Tori Sparks and Leo Cayuela with his "Alegria africana" — two very different proposals, both with blues as their starting point.
