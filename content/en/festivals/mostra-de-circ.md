The Circus School of the Ateneu Popular de 9 Barris has spent years training young people from the district in circus disciplines. The Mostra de Circ is the moment when everything they have learned comes out into the street — literally. An open space, in the middle of the square, with the neighbourhood as audience and the students as protagonists.

---

In some years, the Mostra takes on an extra dimension: the international exchange. In 2025, Plaça Ángel Pestanya hosted the result of an exchange between the Ateneu Circus School and Circus Circuli Stuttgart. Two groups of young people who had spent weeks working together showed what they had learned — and what they had taught each other.

Acrobatics, balance, juggling. Young people performing not to prove themselves but to share.

Documenting it means documenting the continuation of a tradition of popular artistic training that the Ateneu Popular de Nou Barris has maintained since the 1970s.
