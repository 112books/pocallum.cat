La Juganera is not a festival built around a line-up. It is a neighbourhood festival, organised by the community itself, that takes place at Nau Bostik and grows a little each year without losing what makes it what it is: the programme is chosen by the festival committee, and the festival committee is everyone.

---

We have covered La Juganera since its third edition. Each year the programme mixes percussion, street bands, DJs and music that doesn't fit a single label. What never changes is the atmosphere: participatory, with no distance between musicians and audience, and the people of Nou Barris as the main protagonists.

Nau Bostik works as a space. When there is a show, the courtyard transforms, and the July light falls exactly as it should.
