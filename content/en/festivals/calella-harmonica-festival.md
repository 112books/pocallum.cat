The Calella Harmònica Festival is an annual festival dedicated to the harmonica as a cross-genre instrument: blues, jazz, bluegrass, folk. The fourth edition took place on 13 and 14 September 2025 in Calella.

---

Live concerts, masterclasses for all levels, photography exhibitions and a closing paella that proves well-made festivals end with people at the centre, not backstage.

## Our exhibition

At the fourth edition we didn't just go to photograph. We exhibited **"Preses Falses. 40 anys de Blues a Barcelona"** — a photography show that ran alongside the festival's musical programme.

Bringing an exhibition to a harmonica and blues festival closes a circle: concert photographs returning to the concerts, to the same atmosphere that made them possible.
