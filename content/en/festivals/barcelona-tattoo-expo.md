The Barcelona Tattoo Expo is one of the most important tattoo conventions in southern Europe. For many years we worked there as official photographers, documenting artists, competitions and atmosphere. Later the approach shifted: a personal artistic exercise, exploring tattoo aesthetics from a more independent angle.

---

An event that has changed its name several times but has always revolved around the same thing: the art of tattooing, body culture and a very specific community that reunites every October at Fira de Barcelona.

From the early Barcelona Tattoo Expo years to **BAUM** (Barcelona Art Urban Movement), through the **Anime Tattoo Expo** and **Only Tattoo Barcelona** — each edition has had its own personality.
