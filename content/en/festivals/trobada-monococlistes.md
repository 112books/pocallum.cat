A gathering of monocyclists is exactly what it sounds like: people who ride unicycles, who meet, who perform, and who share a space that is not usually theirs. The 23rd edition, in 2017 at the Ateneu Popular de Nou Barris, already carried decades of continuity on its back.

---

The unicycle is one of those circus disciplines practised from a very young age that creates very specific communities. People who have spent years mastering a single wheel — neighbourhood regulars, analogue photographers, weekend acrobats and artists who make their living from one wheel.

In 2017 we documented the gathering in analogue: Leica M6, Jupiter 35mm f/2.8 and Fomapan 200. The grain, the Ateneu's light and the monocyclists did the rest.
