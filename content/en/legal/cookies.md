## This website does not use tracking cookies

pocallum.cat does not use tracking, advertising, or third-party cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to accept.

## Cookie-free web analytics

To measure website traffic we use **GoatCounter**, an analytics system designed specifically to respect user privacy:

- Does not store cookies in your browser
- Does not collect or process personal data
- Does not use fingerprinting
- Does not share any data with third parties
- GDPR-compliant without requiring consent

You can verify this yourself by inspecting your browser's cookies while browsing this site: you will find none from Pocallum.
