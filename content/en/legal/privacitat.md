- Email address

- **Purpose:** to respond to your enquiries and manage any resulting professional relationship
- **Legal basis:** consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR) and legitimate interest in responding to enquiries (art. 6.1.f GDPR)
- **Retention:** for the duration of the professional relationship and until the limitation period for any obligations arising from it

### Web analytics

This website uses **GoatCounter** to measure traffic. GoatCounter is a privacy-respecting analytics system:

- Does not store cookies in your browser
- Does not collect or process personal data
- Does not use fingerprinting
- Does not share any data with third parties
- GDPR-compliant without requiring consent

### Search engine performance

To analyse search engine performance, we use **Google Search Console**, a service by Google LLC. This tool processes aggregated data about how the site appears in Google search results (impressions, clicks, and positions), but does not place cookies or collect personal data from visitors. Data is used solely to improve the site's visibility and content.
