The blog is the soundtrack to what gets photographed. Some entries run long — context, names, dates, opinions. Others are four lines and an album, because it was late, because the work spoke for itself, because nothing needed adding.

It is not a corporate blog. There is no content strategy or editorial calendar. You'll find concerts, festivals, neighbourhood events, camera tests, streets at three in the morning and people who don't know they're being photographed. Since 2010, without interruption.

If you're looking for the author behind the photos, this is where you'll notice there is one.
