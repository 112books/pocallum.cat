## Who is Pocallum

**Pocallum** is the photography service of **Joan Linux Martínez**, a cultural photographer and editor based in Barcelona. Specialising in jazz, blues, flamenco, theatre, dance and performing arts since 2010.

We are not a generalist agency. We are specialists in live cultural photography: the unrepeatable moment of the concert, the performance, the festival.

## Who we work for

### Festivals and events
Organisers of music, theatre, dance and circus festivals who need professional photographic documentation. We have worked with Vijazz, Festival de Blues de Barcelona, Flamenco de Barrio, Arundo Donax, Clot en Blues, Mostra de Circ and many others.

### Artists and musicians
Soloists, bands and companies who need images for press, web, social media or professional portfolio books. Photography that understands music because we know it from the inside.

### Theatre, dance and circus
Companies and directors who need stage documentation and images for production, press and communication.

### Cultural organisations
Concert venues, civic centres, foundations and cultural associations that need photographic coverage of activities and staff.

## Key differentiators

- **15 years of specialisation** in music and performing arts — not a generalist photographer who "also does concerts"
- **Real musical knowledge:** Joan is both a photographer and a cultural activist who genuinely knows the genres he photographs
- **Available light preferred:** no flash when possible, preserving the atmosphere of the live event
- **No fixed rates:** every project deserves a tailored quote
- **Stage presence:** knows how to work in the pit without disrupting the performance
